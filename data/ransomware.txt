Ransomware is a type of malware that encrypts files and demands payment from victims.

WannaCry is a well-known ransomware attack that exploited an SMB vulnerability to spread across networks.

Ransomware can spread quickly across systems and lock important files, making them inaccessible.

It often targets hospitals, organizations, and businesses where data is critical.

Victims are usually asked to pay a ransom to regain access to their files.
